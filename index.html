✨ What is murmur?

murmur is a free, open-source speech-to-text tool designed specifically for macOS. It sits quietly in your menu bar, waiting for one simple gesture: hold a key, talk, and release. Your spoken words appear instantly in any text field, app, or document — as if you had typed them yourself.

But murmur doesn't just transcribe. It cleans up your speech — filling in punctuation, capitalizing proper nouns, and removing filler words like "um" and "like". You get polished, readable text without lifting a finger.

Powered by Apple's Core ML and the Whisper model, everything happens on-device. Your audio never leaves your Mac. No cloud, no waiting, no privacy trade-offs.

Think of it as a keyboard for your voice. Perfect for emails, notes, coding comments, or any time you're faster at talking than typing.

🎤 How to Use murmur

Using murmur is beautifully simple:

  1. Click any text field (email, document, chat bar). Put your cursor where you want the text to appear.
  2. Press and hold the Left Command (⌘) key (you can change this shortcut in settings).
  3. Speak naturally — murmur listens while you hold the key.
  4. Release the key. Your words appear instantly, already punctuated and cleaned up.

No buttons to click, no windows to manage. Just hold, speak, release. It feels like magic, but it's actually sophisticated on-device AI.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Is murmur really free? Yes, absolutely. murmur is open-source and free to use forever. No subscriptions, no hidden costs.

Will my recordings be uploaded somewhere? Never. murmur does all computations locally on your Mac using Apple's Core ML framework. Your voice never leaves your device.

Can I use murmur with multiple apps? Yes, any app that supports text input. murmur types into whatever window is active, exactly like a keyboard.

How accurate is it? Using the same Whisper model that powers ChatGPT's transcription, murmur delivers near-human accuracy, even with accents, jargon, and background noise.

Can I change the trigger key? Absolutely. Open murmur preferences and choose any modifier key (Command, Option, Control, Fn, Caps Lock, or F1-F12 keys).
